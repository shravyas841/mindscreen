# Historical benchmark artifacts

Files under `historical/` predate the final IEEE evaluation package. They use different scripts, test cases, and hardware and do not support the quantitative claims in `mindscreen_conference.tex`.

Authoritative paper outputs are under `mindscreen_code_and_evaluation/mindscreen_code_and_evaluation/evaluation/`:

- `results_fusion.json`
- `results_crisis.json`
- `results_posthoc.json`
- `results_latency.json`

The historical files are retained only for provenance. Do not combine their measurements with the final paper tables.
